09/FAQ

Questions, answered.

Getting to know Duke

What is HustleQ, and who is Duke?

HustleQ is the marketing brain for small business owners. Its strategist, Duke, tells you what to post, why it will work and why now. Before planning anything, he asks about your month: promotions, launches and goals. Then he plans your week with you, explains every recommendation and tells you when something isn’t worth posting. You didn’t start your business to become a marketer, and with HustleQ you don’t have to.

Who is HustleQ for?

Small business owners, solo founders and service providers who know content matters but don’t have the time, the system or the energy to figure it out every week. That includes salons, food businesses, boutiques, coaches, consultants and early stage startups.

How is HustleQ different from other marketing tools?

Most tools hand you templates or generate content when you ask. Duke makes marketing decisions with you: he listens to what’s going on in your business, uses what’s working in your niche right now and explains every recommendation, including what he’d skip. Duke runs on AI, but the point is the judgment behind each recommendation, which comes from years of running marketing for real small businesses.

Do I need to know marketing to use Duke?

No. There’s no jargon, no blank page and nothing to learn first. You answer a few questions about your business, and Duke turns them into a clear plan. Because he always explains why, you’ll understand your marketing better over time.

How Duke works

How does Duke decide what I should post?

Duke looks at three things: what’s working in your niche right now, what you tell him about your business and your month, and your public Instagram profile. Each recommendation shows which of these it’s based on.

What does a weekly plan include?

Duke plans one week at a time, with 1 to 5 content items based on your goals and how much you can realistically make. Each item comes with its format, its goal, the hook, the caption and what to film. You approve the week before it starts.

Does Duke post for me?

No. Duke plans your content and tells you how to make each post. You publish it. If you’d rather have it done with you, that’s the Founding Partner Program.

Can I change what Duke suggests?

Always. Edit anything, ask for another option or tell Duke why something doesn’t fit. He remembers what you tell him, so every plan fits your business a little better than the last.

Which platforms does Duke work with?

Duke starts with Instagram, with more platforms coming.

What do you do with my Instagram handle and business info?

We use your public profile and your answers only to make Duke’s recommendations fit your business. We only look at public information, and Duke never posts on your behalf.

Access and pricing

When can I use Duke?

Duke opens in groups, starting with the waitlist, and we’ll email you as soon as your group is in. We’re not promising a fixed date because we’d rather open well than open fast. Duke First Access members get in before the Free Waitlist. In the meantime, try a preview of Duke.

What’s the difference between the Free Waitlist and Duke First Access?

The Free Waitlist costs nothing and holds your spot. Duke First Access is an optional, one-time, refundable $20 payment, offered after you join, that moves you ahead of everyone on the Free Waitlist, even people who joined before you. Both get an email when Duke opens. First Access gets you in sooner, but it doesn’t guarantee a specific access date.

Is Duke First Access refundable?

Yes, the $20 is fully refundable. Email contact@hustleqai.com any time before you get access and we’ll refund you, no questions asked.

How much will Duke cost?

Joining the Free Waitlist is free. Everyone on the waitlist hears Duke’s pricing before access opens, so there are no surprises. The $20 First Access payment is separate and doesn’t count toward a subscription.
